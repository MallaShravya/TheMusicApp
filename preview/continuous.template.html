Notes or a Line

The same phrase three ways: as separate notes, as notes that overlap, and as one unbroken pitch line that never stops moving. The question is whether the third is still heard as a tune, or only as a siren that happens to pass through the right pitches.

Phrase

Discrete — notes played separately.

Pitch over time

Notation

  • S R G M P D N shuddha, r g d n komal, M# tivra Ma
  • .P an octave down, S' an octave up, - hold, _ rest

Glide is how much of the time between two notes is spent moving rather than resting. At 100 the line never stops; lower it and each note is held before travelling, which is roughly what meend does.
