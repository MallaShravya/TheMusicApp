Sargam Scratchpad

Type swaras, hear them. The parser is src/generator/swara.ts compiled straight out of the app, so the pitches here are the pitches it will produce.

Random notes

Still no scale and no weighting toward any note. One note recurs on a fixed beat and another halfway between — both picked at random, neither of them necessarily Sa — some beats hold or fall silent instead of sounding something new, and a couple of earlier stretches come back later unchanged.

Notation

  • S R G M P D N — shuddha swaras
  • r g d n — komal Re, Ga, Dha, Ni
  • M# or m — tivra Ma
  • .P lowers an octave, S' raises one; both repeat
  • - holds the previous swara another beat, _ is a beat of silence
  • | is a bar line and is ignored
